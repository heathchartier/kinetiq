Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Open the app, tap the 🔒 icon in the header, and choose Delete My Account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • How do I export my data? Your workout history, PRs, measurements, and nutrition logs can be exported as CSV from within the app.
  • I forgot my password. Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen to get a reset link.
  • Is Kinetiq free? Yes — Kinetiq is free to use, with no ads.
